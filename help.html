Frequently Asked Questions
What is Alo AI?
Alo AI is a high-speed intelligence platform built for coding, academic research, generative image synthesis, and Bengali language conversational mastery.
How does the fair usage quota work?
Each user plan includes a rolling window (e.g. Free: 10 messages / 3 hours, Pro: 30 / 3 hours, Max: 50 / 1 hour). When your limit is reached, your quota automatically resets as the window expires.
How can I redeem a code?
Navigate to the Redeem Code page from Settings, enter your 12-character promo code, and your account will immediately upgrade to Pro or Max.
Are Admin accounts rate-limited?
No. Verified admin accounts enjoy unlimited messages, unrestricted image generation, and instant access to all AI models without queuing or rate limits.
